# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

> Inferred from the owner's written brief (16 Nov 2026) and RESEARCH.md. No structured interview was possible in this session; every line below traces to the brief or to a cited listing. Facts marked *open* must not be invented.

## Platform

web

## Users

- **First viewer:** the property manager. She must love the site on first sight; it represents her house.
- **Main audience:** couples and families in Ghana planning an intimate wedding, a traditional (knocking / engagement) ceremony or a bridal event, mostly on phones on mobile data.
- **Second audience:** photographers and videographers scouting a backdrop for bridal portraits and films.

## Product Purpose

Turn interest from TikTok and the rental listings into qualified WhatsApp enquiries (+233 59 611 2287). Success is a conversation started with the date, occasion and guest count already stated.

## Positioning

A private French Rococo-style house in a quiet part of Kumasi: three-tier shell fountain, carved urns, marble statues, chandeliers, a floating staircase, gilt mirrors, and peacocks that live in the garden. Small, private and photogenic, rather than a banquet hall.

## Operating Context

Discovery happens on TikTok (17.2K followers; best content is bridal and fountain films) and on Airbnb, Booking.com and Vrbo. Enquiries and directions happen on WhatsApp. Events carry a separate charge from overnight stays.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page, sections in this order: hero, the house, gallery, occasions, film, enquire, visit. Sticky WhatsApp bar on phones.
- Navigation: logo, three anchors, one WhatsApp button. Minimal footer. Visible copy under ~250 words.
- Stack is fixed by the brief: Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind, Motion, next/image, next/font, sharp.
- *Open:* bedroom count (4 vs 5 across listings), event capacity, exact address, prices. No swimming pool exists in any source.

## Brand Commitments

- Palette from the real photographs: marble ivory, gold leaf, deep peacock green or teal.
- One high-contrast display serif and one clean sans.
- The owner's hard "no" list: AI-slop look, purple or blue gradients, glassmorphism, blobs, emoji or icon rows, three identical cards, centred text over blurred backgrounds, stock photos, lorem ipsum, "dream wedding", "unforgettable", template layouts.
- Owner's bar (verbatim, second message): "should look like it came right out of the best and greatest web design studio in the world. it should look like a million dollars."

## Evidence on Hand

16 host listing photographs in `public/media/images` (placeholders pending the owner's originals; see MEDIA_SOURCES.md). Three TikTok films by ID. No testimonials, press, prices or capacity may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The house is the proof; photographs lead, words follow.
2. One action everywhere: talk to the house on WhatsApp.
3. Fast and calm on a phone on mobile data in Ghana.
4. Only verified facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-operable lightbox, reduced motion respected, 44 px tap targets.
